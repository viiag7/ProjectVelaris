# Quota Requirements

Sending quota exists at two levels:

```text
Environment
    ↓ aggregate usage
Tenant
```

Every submission must pass both levels.

## RF-QUO-001 — Environment Sending Quota

Each Environment must support its own sending quota.

Initially quota windows may include:

- hourly;
- daily;
- monthly.

## RF-QUO-002 — Tenant Aggregate Quota

Each Tenant must have an independent aggregate sending quota.

Tenant usage must equal the sum of usage generated by all of its Environments for the corresponding quota period.

## RF-QUO-003 — Hierarchical Validation

Before accepting a message, the platform must validate:

1. available Environment quota;
2. available Tenant quota.

The submission may be accepted only when both levels have sufficient capacity.

## RF-QUO-004 — Consumption per Recipient

Quota consumption must be based on recipient count rather than Message count.

A Message submitted to three recipients consumes three quota units.

## RF-QUO-005 — Atomic Consumption

Quota reservation or consumption must be concurrency-safe so simultaneous submissions cannot exceed configured limits.

## RF-QUO-006 — Environment Quota Exhaustion

When an Environment reaches its quota, new submissions for that Environment must be rejected.

Other Environments may continue sending while they have Environment quota and the Tenant still has aggregate capacity.

## RF-QUO-007 — Tenant Quota Exhaustion

When a Tenant reaches its aggregate quota, new submissions must be rejected for every Environment belonging to that Tenant.

## RF-QUO-008 — Usage Visibility

The platform must expose, at both Tenant and Environment levels:

- configured limit;
- used amount;
- remaining amount;
- usage percentage;
- period start;
- period end.

## RF-QUO-009 — Usage Thresholds

The platform must be able to detect at least these usage thresholds:

- 80%;
- 90%;
- 100%.

Notification delivery is outside the initial scope.
